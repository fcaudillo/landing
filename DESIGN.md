## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-authority executive transformation. Crafted for seven-to-nine-figure founders, venture partners, and enterprise executives, the aesthetic establishes an uncompromising atmosphere of mastery, intellectual clarity, and discretion.

### Visual Architecture & Movement
- **Dark Luxury Minimalist:** Dominated by deep, non-reflective obsidian planes, razor-thin structural delineation, and deliberate expansive negative space.
- **Atmospheric Restraint:** Avoiding chaotic visual noise; every element exists with calculated intent. Depth is articulated through delicate luminous contrasts, controlled golden reflections, and meticulous typographic hierarchies rather than aggressive ornamentation.
- **Psychological Posture:** Evokes feelings of an exclusive boardroom sanctuary—private, decisive, sovereign, and timelessly commanding.

## Colors

The palette operates in strict tonal layers of deep carbon, crisp crystalline borders, and calculated moments of gold illumination.

### Functional Palette Structure
- **Base Surfaces:**
  - Canvas Deep Obsidian: `#0B0F17` (Global foundation)
  - Elevated Slate Container: `#131A26` (Cards, elevated modal surfaces)
  - Surface Interactive: `#182030` (Hover states and elevated utility modules)
- **Dividers & Structural Borders:**
  - Hairline Rule / Border: `#1F293D` (Low-contrast architectural borders)
  - Focused / Active Border: `#2E3D5B` (Hover/focus strokes)
- **Metallic Gold Accents:**
  - Imperial Champagne: `#D4AF37` (Primary actions, key conversion anchors, decisive focal highlights)
  - Burnished Amber Accent: `#E5C07B` (Subtle secondary indicators, badges, selected states)
  - Muted Gold Veil: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)` (Glows, active states, and selection fields)
- **Monochrome & Typography:**
  - Pure Optical White: `#FFFFFF` (Headings, metric callouts, critical values)
  - Chalk Neutral: `#E1E7F0` (Long-form body, primary text blocks)
  - Slate Muted: `#8E9CAE` (Secondary metrics, field labels, metadata)

## Typography

The typography unites traditional executive gravitas with high-conversion clarity.

- **Display & Headlines (`Newsreader`):** A high-prestige editorial serif with razor-sharp terminal details and dignified proportions. Used for core philosophical statements, hero headlines, executive program titles, and strategic quotes. Display levels must preserve optical letter-spacing tightness to project strength.
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** A geometric, balanced modern sans-serif engineered for effortless scanning and reading comfort across desktop dashboards and mobile consultative workflows.
- **Casing & Micro-copy Rules:** All badges, category trackers, and overline indicators use `label-sm` or `label-md` styled with uppercase transformation (`text-transform: uppercase`) and wide letter spacing (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to evoke curated fine-print luxury.

## Layout & Spacing

This layout system leverages expansive breathing space to project immense value, composure, and order.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid locked within a maximum content container of `1280px`. Margins maintain an unyielding `4rem` (`64px`) boundary; gutters remain steady at `2rem` (`32px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` gutters and `2.5rem` canvas margins.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins.

### Spacing Philosophy
- White space is an active executive asset, not inert void. Vertical stack intervals between major program modules scale from `space-xl` (`40px`) to `space-2xl` (`64px`).
- Internal component gaps preserve an 8px rhythmic foundation, utilizing `space-md` for standard card padding and `space-lg` for premium evaluation tiers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed via architectural surface layering, subtle gold light-bleeds, and crisp micro-borders, entirely rejecting heavy blurred drop-shadows.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Base Layer (`#0B0F17`):** Infinite deep slate ground. Holds full-bleed sections and primary backdrop structure.
2. **Surface Tier 1 (`#131A26`):** Standard container plane for cohort cards, advisory calendars, and intake dossiers. Outlined with a strict 1px solid stroke of `#1F293D`.
3. **Surface Tier 2 (`#182030`):** Elevated interactive components, flyouts, and dropdown sheets. Defined by a 1px border of `#2E3D5B` accompanied by an ambient, deep-tinted shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(3, 6, 11, 0.8)`.
4. **Hero Accents & Focus Depth:** Conversion-critical cards (such as bespoke retainer tiers) employ a hairline perimeter gradient of `#D4AF37` fading into `#1F293D`, paired with a diffuse, soft gold ambient back-glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 35px -10px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined, subtly softened architectural edge (`roundedness: 1`), conveying precision, stability, and enduring structure.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Base Components:** Standard buttons, input controls, chips, and table cells carry a tight `0.25rem` (`4px`) radius.
- **Large Panels & Cards (`rounded-lg`):** Executive overview cards, testimonial statements, and modal dialogs use `0.5rem` (`8px`).
- **Special Displays (`rounded-xl`):** Hero media envelopes and framed video coaching reviews employ `0.75rem` (`12px`).
- Fully rounded circles (`rounded-full`) are strictly reserved for executive portrait avatars and singular numeric state indicators.

## Components

### Buttons & Call-to-Actions
- **Primary Sovereign CTA:** Champagne gold background (`#D4AF37`), dark slate typography (`#0B0F17`), semi-bold weight. Hover triggers subtle brightening to `#E5C07B` and an ambient gold sheen. Dimensions: `h-12`, padding: `0 1.75rem`.
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Transparent background, hairline border (`1px solid #1F293D`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`). On hover, the border steps up to `#D4AF37` with text shifting to `#E5C07B`.
- **Text Link CTA:** Newsreader or Plus Jakarta Sans with a directional arrow (`→`), underlined with a subtle gold hairline spaced 4px below the baseline.

### Input Fields & Application Dossiers
- **Structure:** Surface background of `#131A26`, 1px border of `#1F293D`, font size `body-md`.
- **States:** Focus replaces border with `#D4AF37` and casts a crisp `0 0 0 1px #D4AF37` ring. Placeholder text uses `#8E9CAE`.
- **Field Labels:** Positioned above inputs using `label-sm`, uppercase, `#8E9CAE`.

### Cards & Program Tiers
- **Private Client Tiers:** Canvas set to `#131A26`, wrapped with a precise `1px solid #1F293D`. Padding scales to `space-xl` (`40px`).
- **Featured Retainer Card:** Elevated with a top hairline accent of `2px solid #D4AF37` and a muted badge pinned to the top right reading `"BY INVITATION ONLY"`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Executive Badges:** Low-saturation slate tint (`rgba(31, 41, 61, 0.6)`), border `1px solid #1F293D`, text in `label-sm` (`#E1E7F0`).
- **Active / Verified Status:** Micro amber dot indicator (`6px x 6px`, `#D4AF37`) nestled beside tracking label with `margin-right: 0.5rem`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** Crisp square (`18px x 18px`), `0.25rem` corner radius, `#131A26` background, `#1F293D` border. Checked state fills with `#D4AF37`, featuring a `#0B0F17` geometric check icon.
- **Selection Rows:** Interactive list options that encapsulate radio mechanics into full-width cards with smooth 150ms border transitions upon hover and selection.

### Custom Component: The Executive Metric Callout
- Monolithic numeric stat block displaying founder outcomes (e.g., "$120M+", "3.4X Exit"). Numerical value rendered in `Newsreader` (56px) in `#FFFFFF`, underscored by a 24px wide horizontal accent rule in `#D4AF37`, followed by a `label-md` description in `#8E9CAE`.
